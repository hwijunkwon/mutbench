Lassa Virus Cell Entry Reveals New Aspects of Virus-Host Cell Interaction.

Torriani G(1), Galan-Navarro C(1), Kunz S(2).

Author information:
(1)Institute of Microbiology, Lausanne University Hospital and University of 
Lausanne, Lausanne, Switzerland.
(2)Institute of Microbiology, Lausanne University Hospital and University of 
Lausanne, Lausanne, Switzerland Stefan.Kunz@chuv.ch.

Viral entry represents the first step of every viral infection and is a 
determinant for the host range and disease potential of a virus. Here, we review 
the latest developments on cell entry of the highly pathogenic Old World 
arenavirus Lassa virus, providing novel insights into the complex virus-host 
cell interaction of this important human pathogen. We will cover new discoveries 
on the molecular mechanisms of receptor recognition, endocytosis, and the use of 
late endosomal entry factors.
