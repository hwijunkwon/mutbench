1. Vopr Virusol. 1993 Jan-Feb;38(1):30-4.

[The localization and preliminary characteristics of antigenic sites (B 
epitopes) in the nucleocapsid protein of the Lassa virus].

[Article in Russian]

Vladyko AS, Bystrova SI, Malakhova IV, Kunitskaia LIa, Lemeshko NN, Kras'ko AG, 
Kozhich AT, Lukashevich IS.

Nucleocapsid protein of Lassa virus contains 4 epitope sites. Three of them were 
localized in amino acid position 123-1 27, 337-346, and 518-527. Monoclonal 
antibody 1108 specific to Lassa virus NP-protein reacted with 123-127 synthetic 
peptide in solid-phase ELISA and precipitated nucleocapsid proteins of Machupo, 
Tacaribe, and Mopeia arenaviruses in RIP.
