A New Vaccine Candidate Expressing JUNV GP1-GP2 Against Argentine Hemorrhagic 
Fever Based on Baculovirus Surface Display.

With the aim of developing a new vaccine candidate using a recombinant 
baculovirus (rBV) expressing the Junín virus (JUNV) GP1 and GP2 proteins on its 
surface, a pBacPAK9 vector containing the BV-GP64 signal peptide, the JUNV 
GP1-GP2 and the transmembrane domain of the vesicular stomatitis virus G (VSV-G) 
protein were synthesized. This plasmid was co-transfected with the bApGOZA 
bacmid into Sf9 cells. The rBV was recovered from the supernatant. Protein 
expression was confirmed with a human serum by Western blot and with an anti-GPC 
monoclonal antibody by IFI staining and virometry. In an ELISA test, mice 
immunized with rBV exhibited specific antibody levels that also showed 
neutralizing properties in a plaque reduction test compared to controls. Guinea 
pigs immunized with rBV and challenged with JUNV showed partial protection 
compared to the Candid#1 vaccine. We have developed an rBV surface display 
platform to validate a potential JUNV vaccine candidate.
