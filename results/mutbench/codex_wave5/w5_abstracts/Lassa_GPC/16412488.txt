A recombinant Yellow Fever 17D vaccine expressing Lassa virus glycoproteins.

Bredenbeek PJ(1), Molenkamp R, Spaan WJ, Deubel V, Marianneau P, Salvato MS, 
Moshkoff D, Zapata J, Tikhonov I, Patterson J, Carrion R, Ticer A, Brasky K, 
Lukashevich IS.

Author information:
(1)Department of Medical Microbiology, Center for Infectious Diseases, Leiden 
University Medical Center, Leiden, The Netherlands.

The Yellow Fever Vaccine 17D (YFV17D) has been used as a vector for the Lassa 
virus glycoprotein precursor (LASV-GPC) resulting in construction of 
YFV17D/LASV-GPC recombinant virus. The virus was replication-competent and 
processed the LASV-GPC in cell cultures. The recombinant replicated poorly in 
guinea pigs but still elicited specific antibodies against LASV and YFV17D 
antigens. A single subcutaneous injection of the recombinant vaccine protected 
strain 13 guinea pigs against fatal Lassa Fever. This study demonstrates the 
potential to develop an YFV17D-based bivalent vaccine against two viruses that 
are endemic in the same area of Africa.
