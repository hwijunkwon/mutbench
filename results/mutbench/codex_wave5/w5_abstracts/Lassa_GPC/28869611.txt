1. Nat Med. 2017 Oct;23(10):1146-1149. doi: 10.1038/nm.4396. Epub 2017 Sep 4.

Human-monoclonal-antibody therapy protects nonhuman primates against advanced 
Lassa fever.

There are no approved treatments for Lassa fever, which is endemic to the same 
regions of West Africa that were recently devastated by Ebola. Here we show that 
a combination of human monoclonal antibodies that cross-react with the 
glycoproteins of all four clades of Lassa virus is able to rescue 100% of 
cynomolgus macaques when treatment is initiated at advanced stages of disease, 
including up to 8 d after challenge.
