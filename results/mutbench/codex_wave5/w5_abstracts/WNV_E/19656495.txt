DNA vaccines expressing retrovirus-like particles are efficient immunogens to 
induce neutralizing antibodies.

Bellier B(1), Huret C, Miyalou M, Desjardins D, Frenkiel MP, Despres P, Tangy F, 
Dalba C, Klatzmann D.

Author information:
(1)UPMC Univ Paris 06, UMR7211, F-75013 Paris, France.

We aimed at improving DNA vaccination efficiency for inducing neutralizing 
antibodies. We used plasmids encoding Gag of MLV and envelope proteins of VSV or 
WNV. Upon in vivo injection, they generate retrovirus-derived VLPs pseudotyped 
with these envelopes expressed in their wild-type conformation. We show that 
these plasmo-retroVLPs induce potent humoral responses, the efficacy of which 
could be improved by co-administration of DNA encoding adjuvant cytokines. 
Antibodies against VSV or WNV were detected earlier than with plasmids not 
generating VLPs, and had higher neutralizing activities. These results highlight 
the potential of this approach for vaccination strategies aiming at neutralizing 
antibody induction.
