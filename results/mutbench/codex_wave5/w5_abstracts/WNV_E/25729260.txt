The first reported case of West Nile encephalitis in Korea.

Hwang J(1), Ryu HS(1), Kim H(1), Lee SA(1).

Author information:
(1)Department of Neurology, Asan Medical Center, University of Ulsan College of 
Medicine, Seoul, Korea.

West Nile encephalitis was first identified in 1937, but until now, it was never 
diagnosed in Korea. A 58-yr-old Korean man was admitted with headache and 
cognitive dysfunction. The patient had been on a business trip in Guinea. 
Cerebrospinal fluid (CSF) showed pleocytosis. The patient complained of both leg 
weakness,and arachnoiditis and myelitis were observed on lumbar magnetic 
resonance imaging (MRI). A specific neutralizing antibody for West Nile virus 
was positive in serum. After a treatment with interferon-α 3mu, follow up CSF 
findings recovered completely after 3 months later. The first case of West Nile 
encephalitis in Korea was imported from Guinea, and was cured successfully.
