Titration and neutralizing antibody quantification by focus forming assay for 
Powassan virus.

The development of high-throughput assays measuring Powassan virus (POWV) 
lineage I and II represents an important step in virological and immunological 
studies. By adapting focus-forming assays previously optimized for West Nile 
virus and Zika virus, this protocol is able to determine viral load, evaluate 
antivirals, and measure neutralizing antibodies. Although limited by its 
requirement of a detection antibody, this protocol includes a rapid and 
high-throughput assay for measuring viral titer. By utilizing a baby hamster 
kidney cell line and a 96-well plate format, this protocol allows for more 
sensitivity in the detection of POWV lineage I. For complete details on the use 
and execution of this protocol, please refer to Stone et al. (2022).
