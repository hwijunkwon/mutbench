First serological evidence of West Nile virus activity in horses in Serbia.

Lupulovic D(1), Martín-Acebes MA, Lazic S, Alonso-Padilla J, Blázquez AB, 
Escribano-Romero E, Petrovic T, Saiz JC.

Author information:
(1)Scientific Veterinary Institute "Novi Sad," Novi Sad, Serbia.

West Nile virus (WNV), the most widely distributed flavivirus worldwide, has 
lately reemerged in Europe, causing worrisome outbreaks in humans and horses. 
Serological analysis by enzyme-linked immunoassay and plaque reduction 
neutralization test showed for the first time in Serbia that 12% of 349 horses 
presented specific neutralizing WNV antibodies, which in one case also 
cross-neutralized Usutu virus (USUV). This is the first time that anti-USUV high 
neutralizing antibody titers are reported in horses. All these data indicate 
that WNV and USUV are circulating in the region and advise on the convenience of 
implementing surveillance programs.
