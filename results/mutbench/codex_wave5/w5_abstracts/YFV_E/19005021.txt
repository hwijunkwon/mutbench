Use of a surrogate chimeric virus to detect West Nile virus-neutralizing 
antibodies in avian and equine sera.

Komar N(1), Langevin S, Monath TP.

Author information:
(1)CDC-NCZVED-DVBID-ADB, 3150 Rampart Rd., Fort Collins, CO 80521, USA. 
nkomar@cdc.gov

A chimeric yellow fever virus/West Nile virus (WNV) was compared to WNV alone as 
a biosafety level 2 reagent in the plaque reduction neutralization test for 
determining WNV infection histories. Concordance was 96.3% among 188 avian and 
equine serum samples. Neutralizing antibody titers were frequently more than 
twofold lower with the chimera.
