# Phase 2 Statistics Review

1. **PASS - `omega^2` interpretation correctness.** The manuscript correctly identifies `omega^2 = 0.296` as the evaluation-level largest modeled component, while also disclosing the comparable within-cell residual and the more conservative cell-level estimate (`0.234`) as the better inferential reference. The caveat is not hidden: the results text says the evaluation-level value is mechanically higher and that the cell-level value is more conservative. Evidence: `chapters_en/ch4_results.tex:354`, `chapters_en/ch4_results.tex:383`, `chapters_en/ch3_methods.tex:813`, `chapters_en/ch3_methods.tex:816`.

2. **PASS - Paired vs unpaired test choice.** Same-fold comparisons use paired procedures: nested-LOPO uses paired bootstrap and sign-flip permutation, sliding-window additions use paired Wilcoxon, and Wave 4 uses paired Wilcoxon for 2-core vs recomputed 4-core on the same labeled folds. Cross-panel Layer A' vs Wave 4 is correctly treated as unpaired/descriptive Welch because target identity and label provenance differ. Evidence: `chapters_en/ch3_methods.tex:844`, `chapters_en/ch4_results.tex:197`, `chapters_en/ch4_results.tex:816`, `review/2026-05-02/wave5_replacement/lap_lopo_report.md:24-26`.

3. **PASS - Multiple-comparisons control.** The manuscript separates exploratory ANOVA decomposition from confirmatory/enrichment claims and applies Bonferroni over the searched 780 scoring-detector combinations for vaccine-escape tests. It also distinguishes nominal SARS-CoV-2 enrichment from Bonferroni-surviving H3N2/HIV-1 and notes 8,580-test sensitivity does not change tiering. Evidence: `chapters_en/ch3_methods.tex:825`, `chapters_en/ch4_results.tex:939`, `chapters_en/ch4_results.tex:945`, `chapters_en/ch5_discussion.tex:101`.

4. **PASS - Sign-stability bootstrap interpretation.** Sign stability is treated as sign/re-fit reliability, not as proof of effect size or biological validity. Wave 4 explicitly says the 1/28 expanded stability result is sign-fitting instability, and Wave 5 reports perfect sign stability while still concluding the signal direction is negative on Layer A' and therefore does not validate extension. Evidence: `chapters_en/ch4_results.tex:816`, `chapters_en/ch4_results.tex:820`, `review/2026-05-02/wave5_replacement/lap_lopo_report.md:5-7`.

5. **WARN - MCC vs precision@20 reporting consistency.** The manuscript generally separates metric scopes: MCC is the headline Stage 2 metric, while P1/P6 include top-20 precision as a secondary/null-calibrated metric. However, Layer A' reports mean MCC `-0.055` and precision@20 `0.005`, and the active prose uses this correctly as provenance sensitivity rather than as MCC-equivalent validation; the risk is that the abstract still emphasizes search-space reduction without carrying the precision@20 fragility of Layer A' into the first-page narrative. Evidence: `chapters_en/ch3_methods.tex:790-807`, `chapters_en/ch4_results.tex:804`, `chapters_en/ch4_results.tex:820`, `front_en/abstract.tex:11`.

6. **PASS - Sample-size justification and small-cluster caveats.** The active text repeatedly acknowledges the small `n=11`/`n=12` regime, uses pathogen-cluster and alternative bootstrap/Bayesian frames, and states fixed-effect inference does not generalize to all RNA viruses. It also flags 11 pathogens as above the practical minimum but below 20-30 stable random-effect levels. Evidence: `chapters_en/ch3_methods.tex:855-860`, `chapters_en/ch5_discussion.tex:179`, `chapters_en/ch5_discussion.tex:199-205`, `chapters_en/ch4_results.tex:820`.

7. **PASS - Wave 5 cross-panel Welch caveat enforcement.** The manuscript and Wave 5 report label the Welch test as cross-panel/descriptive and explicitly reject paired validation because the panels differ in target identity and label provenance. The active prose further frames Layer A' as a bounded sensitivity check, not a panel extension or replacement. Evidence: `review/2026-05-02/wave5_replacement/lap_lopo_report.md:24-36`, `chapters_en/ch4_results.tex:820`.

8. **PASS - Permutation-null framework rigor for P1 and P6.** P1 specifies four increasingly structured nulls and reports global Stouffer failure separately from per-pathogen significance; P6 specifies structural and joint structural-evolutionary nulls, smoothed p-values, predeclared degeneracy criteria, and valid 12/12 nulls. The manuscript correctly states that P6 supports descriptive signal under conditioning, not deployability or independent replication. Evidence: `chapters_en/ch4_results.tex:804`, `chapters_en/ch4_results.tex:812`, `review/2026-04-30/wave3/p6_biological_nulls.md:18-21`, `review/2026-04-30/wave3/p6_biological_nulls.md:146-148`.

9. **PASS - Callability pre-registration vs post-hoc adjustment.** The active prose reports the pre-registered P5 rule, its training-fold-only diagnostics, the default `0/12` callable outcome, and the sensitivity sweep without relaxing the conclusion post hoc. It also notes that permissive settings select P1-negative pathogens, reinforcing rather than evading the failure. Evidence: `chapters_en/ch4_results.tex:806-808`, `review/2026-04-30/wave2/p5_callability.md:31-40`, `review/2026-04-30/wave2/p5_callability.md:111-134`.

10. **WARN - Fairness/correlation framing.** Severity: material but bounded. The manuscript explicitly frames Layer A vs `freq` circularity in the discussion and says frequency-best rankings for HCV/Influenza B/Norovirus should be discounted, so the issue is not buried in generic limitations. The warning is that the active text does not state the remembered cross-pathogen anchor value (`rho = 0.973` between rpb-vs-freq and freq-MCC) and instead foregrounds a per-position point-biserial audit with mean `|rho| = 0.12`; readers may not see the strongest correlation/circularity form unless the exact cross-pathogen statistic is added or explained as a distinct diagnostic. Evidence: `chapters_en/ch5_discussion.tex:16-19`, `chapters_en/ch4_results.tex:945`, `chapters_en/ch5_discussion.tex:175-177`.

**Core-Claim Statistical Reliability Rating: medium.** The core retrospective claim that scoring performance is pathogen-dependent is statistically well supported within the active 11-pathogen panel: the `omega^2` result survives cell-level, ANCOVA, wild-cluster, phylogenetic block, mixed-effects, and Bayesian robustness frames, and multiple-comparison handling for the HIV-1 external anchor is adequate. The rating is not high because the operational cold-start/deployment layer is formally bounded by P1 global null failure, P5 `0/12` callability, Layer A'/Wave 5 non-preservation, small-cluster uncertainty, and weak forward-time transfer for most pathogens. The defensible reading is therefore: strong retrospective benchmark evidence for pathogen-dependent information-source choice, medium reliability for broader operational or prospective claims.
