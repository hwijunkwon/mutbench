## 2. Top 3 highest-risk gaps and concrete prose suggestions

1. **No independent recuration or agreement estimate.** Add a limitation sentence: "The literature-curated position-level annotation table was curated as a single-team evidence synthesis; no duplicate independent curation subset, inter-annotator agreement statistic, or adjudicated disagreement log is available, so label reproducibility is not directly measured."

2. **Source-to-label traceability is not IEDB-grade.** Add a supplementary schema with columns such as `source_id`, DOI/PMID, evidence type, original-vs-review flag, source coordinate, benchmark coordinate, extraction text pointer, assay/context, inclusion rule, exclusion reason if considered, curator, and adjudication status. This would turn the current free-text evidence field into a reproducible audit trail.

3. **Region-derived labels may overstate position-level certainty.** Add a prose caveat near the Layer A definition: "For entries derived from source-reported regions rather than individually assayed residues, labels should be interpreted as region-supported position annotations, not as equivalent to residue-specific experimental evidence." Also report sensitivity excluding or down-weighting region-expanded labels.

## 3. Cherry-pick risk

Yes, replacing 1--3 cited source papers with alternative same-topic papers could plausibly change the 309 listed positions, especially where a source contributes many positions or where region-to-position expansion is used. The risk is lower for highly replicated, position-specific entries that appear across multiple studies, but higher for pathogen-specific lists drawn from a single canonical paper, region definitions, or literature summaries. The table has 118 unique evidence-source strings, but several sources dominate large blocks: HVR1 and HVR2 contribute 54 HCV positions, Moore & Williamson 2015 contributes 26 HIV-1 entries, Koel 2013 contributes 25 H3N2 entries, and multiple loop/minimal-epitope sources contribute contiguous EV-A71 positions. Replacing one dominant source with a stricter residue-level source, a newer database, or a paper using different coordinate conventions could therefore alter both membership and prevalence. The current manuscript partially contains this risk by disclosing heterogeneity and by using external checks, but the source-selection sensitivity itself is not measured.

## 4. Verdict

**Yellow.** The literature-curated position-level annotation table is defensible as a transparent, frozen, position-level benchmark artifact with unusually explicit downstream caveats about heterogeneity, circularity, and partial external validation. It is not yet defensible as a fully reproducible ground-truth curation process under formal label-reproducibility standards because curator identity/training, independent agreement, adjudication, per-label uncertainty, IEDB-style evidence structure, and deprecated-label audits are absent.
