## Top 3 framing risks and prose suggestions

1. **LOPO 0/11 is sometimes doing positive narrative work after being declared null-consistent.** The strongest caveat is excellent, but contribution prose still says LOPO "corroborates" pathogen dependence. Suggested replacement: "LOPO 0/11 is reported only as a consistency check; by itself it is expected under permutation and does not provide independent evidence for non-generalization. The load-bearing evidence is the interaction effect plus the HIV-1 escape audit."

2. **Friedman p=0.990 is easy to over-read as proof of no universal method.** The detailed section says it is uninformative and underpowered; the abstract headline is sharper. Suggested replacement: "Friedman testing fails to identify a universally superior combination at n=11 (p=0.990); this non-rejection is underpowered and is interpreted only alongside the large scoring-by-pathogen interaction."

3. **P1 HARD STOP/P4 not-run status is absent from active files.** If this was part of the pre-registered audit trail, active prose should say so. Suggested insertion near `ch4_results.tex:804`: "The pre-registered Wave 1 stopping rule was reached at P1, so P4 was not executed; later P5/P6 audits are separate bounding audits rather than continuation of the stopped branch."
