# codex Deep Critical Review (Phase 5, post-v149, 2026-04-28)

## Findings

### P5-01, area C

- Severity: SERIOUS
- Defect: The 4-feature core / EqualWeight LOPO claim is not reproduced by the implementation described in the available scripts. The dissertation states that EqualWeight applies a training-pathogen directional sign rule before averaging (`chapters_en/ch3_methods.tex:690-695`) and later describes the integration formula as z-scored features with `sign(\hat{s}_f)` (`chapters_en/ch4_results.tex:884-888`). It also states that the 4-feature core retains 97.6% under an EqualWeight top-10% LOPO protocol (`chapters_en/ch4_results.tex:836-837`, `chapters_en/ch6_conclusion.tex:14`). The ablation script, however, computes EqualWeight by z-scoring the target pathogen feature matrix and averaging with uniform positive weights, with no sign vector and no held-out-pathogen training fold (`scripts/feature_ablation.py:71-99`). Its cumulative 4-feature selection loads a global AUC table, ranks features by mean `abs(AUC-0.5)`, and then evaluates the same data, again with no fold-specific feature order or sign fitting (`scripts/feature_ablation.py:199-231`). The Stage 2 EqualWeight scoring implementation also uses min-max normalization and simple averaging, not the stated z-score-plus-sign rule (`scripts/run_stage3_full_detectors.py:180-187`; same pattern in `scripts/run_stage3_benchmark.py:109-116`).
- Why prior audit missed it: Prior reviewers appear to have audited the paper text and the final CSV values, not the train/test semantics of the generator scripts. Because the patched text already added "LOPO", "no paired-test significance", and "training pathogens" caveats, the remaining problem is a code-protocol mismatch rather than an obvious wording overclaim.
- Fix scope: RE-ANALYSIS (1 day)
- Concrete next step: Re-run the 4-feature and 10-feature EqualWeight comparison with a single canonical implementation: for each held-out pathogen, compute feature orientation and cumulative feature order on the other pathogens only, apply the frozen sign/order to the held-out pathogen, and report the fold-level 4-vs-10 MCC ratio with paired bootstrap or at least fold-wise deltas. If the retained fraction is materially different from 97.6%, update abstract, Ch1, Ch4, and Ch6; if it is stable, replace the archived `feature_ablation_results.csv` with the nested-LOPO artifact and cite that provenance.

### P5-02, area D / new

- Severity: MODERATE
- Defect: The EqualWeight vaccine-escape block mixes protocols under a shared "top-5%" label. Chapter 4 labels the multi-source rows as "EqualWeight, top 5% threshold" (`chapters_en/ch4_results.tex:912-915`) and Ch6 cites `escape_validation_adapt.csv` as the EqualWeight integration block source with `threshold=top5` (`chapters_en/ch6_conclusion.tex:109-117`). That CSV contains only SARS-CoV-2 and H3N2 top-5 rows, and its H3N2 row matches the reported 4.012x result (`results/mutbench/escape_validation_adapt.csv:9-12`). The reported HIV-1 EqualWeight row, however, is not in `escape_validation_adapt.csv`; it matches the separate 20-scoring detector-grid artifact where `EqualWeight + CUSUM(d=0.5,h=5.0)` gives enrichment 4.1667 and p=0.003734 (`results/mutbench/vaccine_escape_stage3.csv:2006`; generated by `scripts/run_vaccine_escape_stage3.py:98-129`). The script that creates `escape_validation_adapt.csv` defines escape sites only for SARS-CoV-2 and H3N2 (`scripts/validate_escape_adapt.py:41-61`) and adds top-5 sensitivity rows only for those pathogens (`scripts/validate_escape_adapt.py:206-243`). Thus the table row labelled as an EqualWeight top-5 integration result for HIV-1 is actually a best-detector EqualWeight row from a different search protocol.
- Why prior audit missed it: The headline HIV-1 external anchor is the single-scoring frequency result, so the inconsistent EqualWeight HIV row sits in a secondary block and looks harmless unless one traces row provenance across both CSVs.
- Fix scope: SUPPLEMENTARY (1-4 hours) if corrected as provenance/table cleanup; RE-ANALYSIS (1 day) if the dissertation wants a unified EqualWeight-vs-baseline escape comparison across all three pathogens.
- Concrete next step: Split Table `tab:vaccine_escape_stage3` into two blocks with explicit provenance: (1) full 20x39 single/composite detector-grid results from `vaccine_escape_stage3.csv`; (2) fixed-threshold integration comparison from `escape_validation_adapt.csv`, restricted to the pathogens present in that file. Alternatively, add HIV-1 to `validate_escape_adapt.py` and regenerate the fixed-threshold integration CSV before retaining a three-pathogen EqualWeight top-5 block.

## No-finding areas

- A. Layer A heterogeneity is not a new critical defect: the methods define Layer A as an operationally heterogeneous union (`chapters_en/ch3_methods.tex:280-296`), Ch5 explicitly states the curation-protocol confound (`chapters_en/ch5_discussion.tex:300-310`), and subset re-estimates excluding HCV/Norovirus are reported (`chapters_en/ch5_discussion.tex:289-292`).
- B. Layer C phenotype/cell-line heterogeneity is not hidden: the per-pathogen DMS phenotypes and assay systems are tabled (`chapters_en/ch3_methods.tex:315-343`), and the Layer A vs C divergence is framed as non-identical hotspot dimensions rather than fully independent validation (`chapters_en/ch4_results.tex:561-589`).
- D. Bonferroni denominator reuse is caveat-covered for the main HIV-1 single-scoring anchor: the methods define the per-pathogen 780 family and state the 8,580 cross-pathogen denominator does not change tier assignment (`chapters_en/ch3_methods.tex:884-890`), and the Ch4 caption repeats the 780 and 8,580 interpretations (`chapters_en/ch4_results.tex:921`).
- E. MAFFT `--auto` remains a limitation, not a newly hidden defect: the methods state that algorithm choice may differ across pathogens and that alignment-quality effects were not controlled (`chapters_en/ch3_methods.tex:131-135`). I found no fixed-algorithm re-run artifact, so this is residual audit risk, but it is disclosed rather than structurally missed.
- F. Sample-size and metric-confound risk is addressed beyond text: design heterogeneity is acknowledged (`chapters_en/ch3_methods.tex:809-841`), Ch5 reports technical-covariate sensitivity/ANCOVA and subset checks (`chapters_en/ch5_discussion.tex:289-292`), and the small-sample paragraph reports the n=9 exclusion sensitivity (`chapters_en/ch5_discussion.tex:315-321`).
- G. The single-anchor status of Contribution 3b is explicit: Ch4 calls HIV-1 the primary anchor and labels H3N2/SARS-CoV-2 as self-consistency/exploratory (`chapters_en/ch4_results.tex:921-956`), while Ch6 repeats that external validation is anchored by HIV-1 (`chapters_en/ch6_conclusion.tex:15`, `chapters_en/ch6_conclusion.tex:88-91`).
- H. DMS threshold sweep incompleteness is disclosed: the methods say only four DMS pathogens were available for the 10/20/30% sweep (`chapters_en/ch3_methods.tex:317-319`), and Ch4 repeats "4 of the 6 DMS pathogens" before making the rank-stability statement (`chapters_en/ch4_results.tex:561-566`).
- I. PAHD-R is bounded as future-validation evidence rather than a headline algorithm: Ch4 states that candidate sweeps do not replace the three global modes and require external/time-forward validation (`chapters_en/ch4_results.tex:975-986`), and Ch6 positions PAHD-R as an intermediate synthesis with future validation (`chapters_en/ch6_conclusion.tex:70-74`).
- J. Reproducibility risk is disclosed: the dissertation states the single codebase/snapshot/seed limitation and queues independent reproduction (`chapters_en/ch5_discussion.tex:287`), with code/data availability and seed 42 listed in Ch6 (`chapters_en/ch6_conclusion.tex:96-107`). The highest-risk reproducibility issue I found is not seed sensitivity but the EqualWeight protocol mismatch in P5-01.

## Overall verdict

- Are there any FATAL or SERIOUS findings that block defense? Yes.
- Blocking finding: P5-01.
- There are no FATAL findings. The dissertation is close to defensible on the main ω² and HIV-1 single-scoring anchor, but the 4-feature 97.6% / EqualWeight LOPO claim should be treated as not yet audit-clean until the nested-LOPO implementation is regenerated or the claim is downgraded to a descriptive in-sample ablation result.
